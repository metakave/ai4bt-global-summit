Authoritative answers regarding the AI4BT Global Summit 2026 agenda, attendance criteria, online broadcast format, and delegate toolkits.

The AI4BT Global Summit 2026 (25–27 September 2026 • 8:00 PM – 11:00 PM BST) is a premier 3-day virtual executive summit connecting world-class international AI pioneers with Bangladesh enterprise leaders, CXOs, and industry innovators.

Organized across five foundational pillars, the summit focuses on moving beyond AI hype into pragmatic enterprise transformation—covering agentic ERP workflows, scalable infrastructure, sector-specific deployment across RMG, Banking, and Pharma, and responsible governance through the collective ratification of the Dhaka Declaration 2026.

The summit is organized into three intensive, high-impact evening broadcasts (8:00 PM – 11:00 PM BST):

  • Day 1: The AI-Native Horizon & Leadership Readiness — Executive keynotes on strategic AI visioning, organizational mindsets, and human-AI collaboration frameworks.
  • Day 2: Transforming ERP & Autonomous Agentic Workflows — Live interactive simulations showing multi-agent AI systems automating cashflow prediction, demand forecasting, and supply chain reconciliation across SAP, Oracle, and enterprise architectures.
  • Day 3: AI Governance, Cybersecurity & Dhaka Declaration 2026 — Enterprise compliance frameworks, sovereign data safety, ethical AI guardrails, and the formal ratification of the Dhaka Declaration 2026.
The summit is curated for C-level executives (CEOs, CIOs, CTOs, CFOs, COOs), Managing Directors, Board Members, IT & Digital Transformation Directors, ERP Architects, and forward-thinking entrepreneurs from core industries including Banking & Fintech, RMG & Textiles, Pharmaceuticals, Logistics, Manufacturing, and Enterprise Technology who are responsible for leading AI strategy and operational modernization.
The summit is conducted 100% online through a high-definition interactive virtual broadcast platform featuring live keynote presentations, panel debates, and technical breakout workshops. All confirmed registered delegates receive exclusive post-summit access to on-demand session recordings, speaker presentation decks, and downloadable workshop toolkits.

In addition to full 3-day session access, registered delegates receive the AIVolve Leadership Dynamics Suite—including the benchmarked HARS® (Human-AI Readiness Scan) assessment (free for the first 300 delegates), Leadership & Team Readiness diagnostics, and the AI Coach companion.

Delegates also receive the complete AI4BT Enterprise Transformation Starter Pack: (1) AI Readiness Pulse, (2) AI Opportunity Canvas, (3) AI + ERP Readiness Checklist, (4) Responsible AI Governance Checklist, (5) 90-Day AI Transformation Roadmap, and (6) a private 30-Minute AI4BT 1-on-1 Consultation with executive council leaders.
