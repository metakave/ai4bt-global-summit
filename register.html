Answers & Clarity

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about executive delegate eligibility, registration fees and complementary access, live interactive labs, and summit participation.

01

Who is eligible to register as an Executive Delegate?

The AI4BT Global Summit is curated exclusively for C-level executives, Managing Directors, Board Members, Founders, CIOs, CTOs, CFOs, Heads of Supply Chain, and Senior Transformation Leaders from Bangladesh's core industries — including RMG & Textiles, Banking & Fintech, Pharmaceuticals, Manufacturing, Logistics, Conglomerates, and Technology.

02

What is the registration fee or pricing structure?

Registration fee will be declared soon. It will be a nominal fee and also complementary access will be provided to selective Industry Leaders. Actual registration fee for public will be declared soon.

Delegates who register their interest now will receive priority notification once official fee tiers are announced and will be considered for complimentary executive nomination passes.

03

What are the exact dates and session timings?

The summit takes place across three consecutive evenings: 25, 26, & 27 September 2026, strictly from 8:00 PM to 11:00 PM Bangladesh Standard Time (BST / UTC+6). This evening schedule eliminates workday disruption and allows executives to engage with complete focus.

04

Is the summit online or in-person? How will I join?

The entire summit is broadcast 100% online in ultra-high definition with live interactive stage rooms, breakout lounges, and real-time polling. Verified delegates will receive secure single-click credentials and calendar invitations directly via email.

05

Can multiple executives from the same enterprise attend together?

Yes. We strongly encourage leadership cohorts (e.g., Managing Director, CFO, CIO, and Head of Operations) to participate together to align company-wide transformation strategies. Each member should submit an individual registration, or email info@ai4bt-summit.com for coordinated delegation onboarding.

06

What practical demonstrations and live labs are featured?

The summit features hands-on Agentic ERP Live Simulations demonstrating autonomous procurement (P2P), automated sales order processing (O2C), multi-agent inventory optimization, and cashflow prediction connected to SAP, Oracle, and enterprise ERP architectures.

07

What is the Dhaka Declaration 2026 Ratification on Day 3?

On Day 3 (27 Sept 2026), attending enterprise leaders, global keynote mentors, and industry bodies will formally co-sign the Dhaka Declaration 2026. This milestone charter establishes governance benchmarks, cybersecurity thresholds, and a collective capability roadmap for national enterprise AI leadership.

08

Will session recordings, slides, and playbooks be provided?

Yes. All confirmed VIP delegates receive full access to the digital post-summit vault, containing on-demand recordings of all 3 evenings, keynote slide decks, CXO diagnostic pulse reports, and the complete AI Transformation Architecture Playbook.

09

How soon will I receive registration confirmation?

Immediately upon submitting the registration form, you will receive an on-screen confirmation summary. Our executive desk reviews corporate credentials within 24–48 hours, after which you will receive your Official VIP Delegate Pass with calendar (.ics) sync files.

10

Who can I contact for speaker, corporate, or technical inquiries?

For speaker coordination, media partnerships, corporate delegations, or technical inquiries, please email our Secretariat at info@ai4bt-summit.com or use the Delegation Support contact box above.
